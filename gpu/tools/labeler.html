    How to label

    Open a WAV (capture one with tools/capture-song.sh). The picture shows what rises in the sound: a drum is a vertical bar through many rows, the bass stays low, a held note is dark. Do one pass per drum type at 0.5× and tap each hit; the tap snaps to the sharpest onset of that kind within a fraction of a second. Then turn on the detector's marks and fix what you missed. Export, and put the file in gpu/labels/.

    K T S H Ctap kick, tom, snare, hat, cymbal (hold Shift for a ghost note: soft, not asked of the detector)
    Spacetap the highlighted drum (one pass per drum: pick it, then tap on Space)
    Enter / Pplay / pause
    ← →back / forward 1 s (Shift: 5 s)
    1–4speed 1×, 0.75×, 0.5×, 0.35×
    Backspace / Ctrl+Zundo the last mark, region or edit
    Eedit: click the picture to add a mark of the highlighted drum, click one of your marks to delete it, click a detector mark in its lane to accept it
    Vshow or hide the detector's marks (hollow); bright ones have no mark of yours near them
    I Ostart and end of the excerpt you label (Shift+I clears); L loops it
    Nstart, then end, a stretch with no drums in it (counts false hits)
    mouseclick the picture to seek, drag to pan, wheel to zoom
    Ctrl+Sexport; the work is also kept in this browser as you go
